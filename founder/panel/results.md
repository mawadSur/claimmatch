## What would flip a no

- Let me open that TikTok no-proof settlement and the official claim form with no account, no address, no phone — and stay free, not a bait-and-switch subscription.
- If it already knew a store or account I actually use, showed me the official notice that matches, and filed it before I forgot. A list I have to hunt through is just another tab I will never open again.
- If I could browse without an account and a friend actually got a real check from a claim they found here.
- Nothing, honestly. Top Class Actions is free and I only ever look up one claim.
- If I could see a real payout of a couple hundred dollars on a claim that actually applied to me and took two minutes, not a scavenger hunt for $10.
- Search the named breach with no account and land on the official claims-administrator notice, not another signup wall.
- If it showed me a no-proof claim for a store or bank I actually use and I could tell in ten seconds I qualify, without making an account.
- If I could search my named breach with no account and no personal info, and it only showed me the official administrator link.
- If I could browse and open the official administrator's claim form with no account and no personal information stored by you, and I already recognized that administrator's name.
- If I could check that one TikTok settlement against an official claims page I already recognize, with no account, and I actually saw my bank or phone company named on the notice.
- Put the official claim form for the settlement I googled right in front of me with no account and no future fee on that one claim. I'm not paying a subscription for something I do once.
- If a notice I actually got in the mail from my bank or the court named this site, I'd maybe look. A random website promising free help is a no.
